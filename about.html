— About Us

Feyti Medical Group

Feyti Medical Group, a Ugandan-American company, is revolutionizing the landscape of drug safety and pharmacovigilance with the transformative power of artificial intelligence (AI). Since its inception, Feyti has embarked on an unwavering pursuit of innovation, harnessing AI to enhance clinical research, streamline compliance, and usher in a new era of ethical and efficient drug development.

— Our Vision

To become the world’s leading AI-powered CRO.

Shaping the future of healthcare through groundbreaking innovation that puts patients and communities first.

— Our Mission

To revolutionize clinical research and compliance with AI.

Ushering in a new era of efficient and ethical drug development that benefits patients across Africa and beyond.

— Our Journey

Our Journey with AI:
A Trailblazing Path

From the outset, Feyti Medical Group has embraced AI as a cornerstone of its success, developing cutting-edge AI-driven platforms for clinical research and pharmacovigilance.

2017

National Champions at the MTN App Challenge

Feyti’s innovative spirit and potential were recognized when it emerged victorious at the MTN App Challenge, showcasing its ability to tackle complex healthcare challenges using technology.

2020

Expansion into Pharmacovigilance and Regulatory Affairs

Demonstrating its commitment to comprehensive drug safety solutions, Feyti expanded its expertise into Pharmacovigilance and Regulatory Affairs Compliance and Management.

2022

Investment in AI and Automation Research

Recognizing the transformative potential of AI in drug safety, Feyti made a strategic investment in AI and automation research for Clinical and Regulatory Compliance Management.

2023

Transformation into a Fully Managed CRO

Solidifying its global reach, Feyti Medical Group transformed into a fully managed CRO, expanding services to clients across three major continents — Americas, Africa, and Asia.
